    8. Recommendations

    1. Full-service operators: treat app listings as a loss leader to limit, not grow: lower commission, a restricted app menu, and moving repeat customers to Self-Delivery or direct ordering.
    2. Aggregator-heavy cuisines (Chicken Dishes, Japanese, Kebabs/Mediterranean): build self-delivery capacity. Pizza already routes 45% of delivery through its own fleet.
    3. Policy: a commission cap is the largest lever tested, but a 20% cap alone does not make Full-service app orders profitable.
    4. Central Auckland: the case for support rests on its 0.99 growth multiplier; its channel mix and margins match other areas.

    9. Limitations

    • One monthly snapshot; growth is a scenario.
    • All costs are percentages of revenue, so price changes cannot be tested; a menu markup would leave margins unchanged in this model.
    • Delivery splits are uniform within a cuisine, which suggests modelled or stratified data. Findings describe this dataset.
    • The project plan cites 520 branches; the file holds 1,696. All figures use the file.

    Definitions

    Aggregator dependence = (Uber Eats + DoorDash orders) / MonthlyOrders. In-store reliance = in-store orders / MonthlyOrders. Diversification = 100 x (1 - normalised Herfindahl index over four channel shares). Tier 2 = negative net profit on both platforms.
